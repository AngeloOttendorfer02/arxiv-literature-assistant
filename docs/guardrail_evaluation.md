# Guardrail evaluation (S-13)

Test sets: `eval/guardrails` (own wording, split into tuning and test, see its README). Input cases: 105 tuning, 99 test; no LitSearch questions (DATA_DIR not available), harmless cases are our own. Embedding model `ibm-granite/granite-embedding-english-r2`. Generated by `scripts/evaluate_guardrails.py`.

Rule: thresholds are chosen on the tuning split only, as the highest DSR with at most 5 percent false refusals (for the evidence guard: clean passages removed). The test split is read with the chosen values. Intervals are 95 percent Wilson intervals; with about 25 cases per group they are wide.

## Notes

- Replay check: 0 cases where the replayed category differs from the real `check_input` at the default thresholds.
- A threshold cannot fix attacks whose Prompt Guard score is near zero. The evidence guard scores papers that merely discuss attacks like real attacks, so they are reported separately and do not enter the cap.
- The evidence figures use our own clean passages. A sample of real chunks (docs/evidence_real_sample.md, D069) scores higher than they do, so a low threshold chosen here would remove many real sources.
- The cases are our own wording, written by one person, and the patterns of D066 were written after the tuning cases were known. Treat the figures as an estimate for this set, not as general detection rates.
