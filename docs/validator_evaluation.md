# Sentence validator evaluation (S-15)

69 tuning and 61 test cases from `eval/guardrails/validator_cases.jsonl` (own passages and answer sentences; faithful, paraphrased, and manipulated by a changed number or year, a negation, an added claim or a citation of the wrong passage). NLI model `dleemiller/ModernCE-large-nli`. Generated by `scripts/evaluate_validator.py`.

Rule: the entailment threshold is chosen on the tuning split only, as the highest recall with at most 5 percent faithful sentences removed (sentences that need two passages do not enter the cap, see the notes). The contradiction threshold only changes the reported reason, not whether a sentence is removed. Intervals are 95 percent Wilson intervals; with about 50 cases per split they are wide.

## Notes

- Replay check: 0 cases where the replayed status differs from the real `check_sentence` at the default thresholds.
- Latency of `check_sentence` per sentence on CPU with two cited passages: median 178.3 ms, p95 375.2 ms.
- A sentence that combines facts of two cited passages is judged against each passage alone (one supporting source is enough, D030), so such a sentence can be removed although it is true. The cases of kind `faithful_combined` show how often; they do not enter the cap of the threshold rule because no threshold keeps them.
- The sentences are written by one person in the style of the answers, not produced by the language model. Real answers may be harder or easier. Treat the figures as an estimate for this set.
