# S-09 NLI check

1,034 synthetic pairs from 150 random LitSearch abstracts (D-03 corpus, seed 42). Per abstract: one of its sentences (entail, must pass), a sentence of the abstract negated (negation) or with a changed number (number), and a sentence of the most similar other abstract by granite embedding (wrong_paper); the last three must fail. Premise: the abstract (short) or the abstract shuffled with two random ones (long). A pair passes when the entailment probability reaches the threshold. Scores on GPU in fp16, batches of 32, latency on the CPU in fp32 with 4 threads (median of 15 pairs). Generated by `scripts/nli_check.py`; basis of D057.
